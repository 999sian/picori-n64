# romfs/

Drop your **`baserom.gba`** here (USA, SHA1 `b4bd50e4131b027c334547b4524e2dbbd4227130`).

It is copyright Nintendo/Capcom and **not shipped** — supply your own dump. The
build DFS-embeds it into `picori.z64`; the engine reads game data/assets from it
over the PI bus at runtime. `*.gba` here is gitignored.
